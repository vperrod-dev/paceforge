## What to improve

1. **Gate bike quality by the same readiness rules as running.** Readiness under 40 or HRV LOW → the menu is `recovery-45` or `endurance-90` capped at IF ≤ 0.65, not sweet spot. The 2x20 would have been a great choice on Tuesday's green-readiness day instead.

3. **Wear the HR strap on the trainer.** Power says what you did; HR says what it cost. With both we get aerobic decoupling on 20-min blocks — the single best free signal of whether sweet spot is genuinely sub-threshold for you — and cross-checks on days like today when readiness and power targets disagree.
4. **One quality slot per day, total, across sports.** If a run day already went moderate (like this morning), the bike option that evening is easy spin or nothing. Doubling is fine when both halves respect the plan — today neither did.
