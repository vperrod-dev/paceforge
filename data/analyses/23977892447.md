## What to improve

- **Respect the readiness signal.** On a 36.7/low-readiness Recovery day,
  cap effort at conversational HR (roughly <150 bpm, your Z2/low-Z3 line)
  for the whole run — this one crossed into Z4/Z5 by km 4, undoing the
  purpose of an easy day.
- **Cadence still below target.** 159 spm avg is well under your current
  168-172 spm watch target (set 2026-08-10); as pace picked up you lengthened
  stride (135→148 cm) instead of quickening turnover, which is the
  over-striding pattern the target is meant to correct. Focus quick, light
  steps rather than reaching with the stride, especially in the back half.
- **If it's meant to be easy, don't negative-split it.** A 4:58→4:20/km drift
  with rising HR is a workout, not an easy run — either label/plan it as a
  progression run when you want that stimulus, or hold pace flat and let HR
  stay low when the goal is recovery.
