## What to improve

- **Cap effort by HR, not feel, on hilly/interrupted routes.** On terrain like
  this, "easy" needs a hard ceiling (~150 bpm) rather than a pace target — km 6
  shows feel alone let HR run to 91% of max while the split objectively was slow.
- **Cadence is the biggest lever here.** Even your continuously-run kilometres
  sat at 132–159 spm, below the 170–180 economical band; on the walk-heavy
  splits it fell to 85–116. Cue quick, light steps whenever you are actually
  running, and treat this kind of stop-start route as time-on-feet, not a pace
  workout.
- **Route choice matters more than willpower.** Save genuinely flat, uninterrupted
  routes for prescribed easy/long days so the prescribed pace and effort can
  actually be held — this route's terrain (37 m gain, frequent stops) makes an
  easy execution close to impossible regardless of intent.
