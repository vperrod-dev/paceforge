## What to improve

- **Run easy runs in the window.** Treadmill makes this trivial: set 4:45–4:50/km
  (12.4–12.6 km/h) and leave it. If the belt pace feels slow, that's the workout
  working.
- **Cadence is the real economy limiter: 157.6 spm average** with a 1.43 m stride
  at 4:28/km — classic over-striding signature, and your ground contact time of
  265 ms backs it up (over 250 ms is on the heavy side). Aim for ~165 spm at easy
  pace as the next step (about +5%): shorter, quicker steps, feet landing under
  your hips. The treadmill is the perfect place to drill it — count 30 right-foot
  strikes in 20 seconds, or run a few minutes each km with a 165 bpm metronome.
- **Protect the progression instinct for quality days.** The negative-split habit
  (4:40 → 4:20) is a great skill — spend it on progressive tempos, not recovery-week
  easy runs.
