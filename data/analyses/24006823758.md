## Effect on your profile

Aerobic TE of 3.0 is a solid moderate stimulus, but anaerobic TE of only 0.2 is low for a
repetition-pace session — it confirms the reps weren't hit hard enough to reach real
neuromuscular/speed overload. A session that lands consistently inside the 3:41–3:57/km
band would typically show anaerobic TE well above 1.0. Cadence during the reps themselves
averaged 168–173 spm (close to the 174 spm watch target) even though pace was slow — so
the shortfall was stride power, not turnover, and cadence discipline is already close to
where it needs to be.

## What to improve

- **Hit the number, not the feel**: 8 of 10 reps were run 3–19 sec/km slower than the
  target band. On a treadmill the fix is mechanical — dial the belt to 15.2–16.3 km/h
  (3:41–3:57/km) for each rep instead of pacing by effort, so "fast" actually means fast.
- **Rep-to-rep consistency**: paces swung from 3:53/km to 4:16/km with no clean fade
  pattern — treat each rep as its own effort and reset fully in the 60s walk, rather than
  letting early reps run conservative and late reps scattered.
- Per-rep distance varied 201–233m for a nominal 200m — treadmill belt-based distance
  is imprecise over short accelerations, so read these splits by pace/time, not exact
  metres.
