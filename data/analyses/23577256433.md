## What to improve

- **Reschedule the strides, don't skip them permanently.** They didn't
  happen at all today. Once readiness recovers (watch HRV trending back to
  "balanced" and body battery above ~60), slot 4–6 relaxed strides onto the
  next easy day rather than folding them into this week's total.
- **Respect the readiness gate, but log it as a recovery jog, not a target
  miss.** Given HRV was unbalanced and readiness was 45, an 18-minute
  truncated jog was the correct call — treat it as "listened to the body"
  rather than a session to make up.
- **Cadence is low for this pace band.** 155–163 spm (avg 159.7) sits well
  under the 170–180 spm typical target, and it's paired with a fairly long
  ground contact time (270.9ms). At sub-5:00/km pace that combination
  usually means slight overstriding rather than efficient turnover — stride
  length averaged 1.25m, which is doing more of the speed work than cadence
  is. Vertical ratio (7.89%) is genuinely efficient, so this isn't a
  mechanics breakdown, but a quicker-feet cue (aim ~165–170 spm on the next
  easy run) would trim ground contact time and reduce impact loading as
  pace picks up.
- **Watch the humidity tax.** 94% humidity at a mild 15°C is enough to push
  HR 5–10 bpm higher than the same pace on a dry day. On days like this,
  target HR (stay under ~146, top of zone 3) rather than the pace band, and
  expect the easy pace to sit a touch slower than 4:58/km without it being
  a fitness problem.
