## What to improve

1. **Easy means the pace on the card, not the pace that feels respectable.** Treadmill makes this trivial: set 4:55/km (12.2 km/h) and don't touch it. If HR drifts past 140 after 20 min, slow down, don't tough it out.
2. **Cadence is the big economy lever.** 157 spm at 1.40 m stride at easy pace is over-striding. You proved 176+ spm is available in the strides — bring the easy-run baseline up ~5% to 164–166 spm with a shorter step. Use the watch's cadence field or a 165 bpm metronome for the first 10 min.
3. **Strides at target pace.** 3:30–3:45/km is ~17 km/h on the belt — set it, hold 20 s, step to the rails to recover. Speed from turnover, like your last two strides, not from reaching.
