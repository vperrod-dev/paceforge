## Rationale

VO2max 53.4 and a resting HR of 51 put the aerobic engine well ahead of the running volume behind it (13.5 km/week recently) — the Base phase is sized to grow mileage before asking for pace. Fitness 2.0 flags 'grey-zone training' as the top limiter (16% true easy vs. 51% moderate, driven by HYROX cross-training at 120-148 bpm most days) and a habitual fast-start fade in race history (positive-split ratio 1.027) — this plan leans on progressive and race-pace long runs specifically to retrain that pacing pattern, and keeps quality days genuinely hard so the easy days can afford to be genuinely easy.

## Coaching notes

- Guard the easy days — HYROX cardio already supplies plenty of moderate-intensity work; running easy at true conversational effort is where this plan's biggest gap gets closed.
- Cadence on recent runs averages 155-160 spm; use every stride/speed session (cadence targets of 172-174 are built into the Peak-phase speed and VO2max sessions) to nudge it up.
- Practice negative splits deliberately in the progressive and race-pace sessions — race history shows a fast-start/fade pattern (ratio 1.027) that these sessions exist to correct.
- Log RPE on the HYROX cross-training sessions — most are unrated, and they're the only way that load gets counted alongside the running plan.
- If training_readiness or HRV drop into clearly low territory close to a hard day, take the easy option — current readiness (47, Garmin status Recovery) is worth watching week to week.

### Week 1 — Base (14.4 km)

_Base week 1 of 3: laying aerobic foundation and durable form — economy work now makes every later phase cheaper._

- **Tue 2026-07-28 — 3km Easy + 6 Strides** (2.8 km, ~13 min)
  - **Why:** Easy volume plus neuromuscular priming — strides teach fast, relaxed turnover without fatigue, and set up the next quality day.
  - **Structure:** 2.2 km easy @ 4:35-5:03/km → 6× (Stride — fast & relaxed @ 3:30-3:45/km / 45s walking rest @ 4:35-5:03/km)
  - **Feel:** Easy throughout; strides are fast and springy, ~90% effort, never sprinted.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Strides feeling forced or heavy-legged? Cut them — the easy running is the session; strides are a bonus.
  - **Form cue:** Strides are turnover practice: quick light steps, arms driving straight back, eyes up.
  - **Coach's note:** Your last dozen runs average ~158 spm, below the 170+ turnover this plan is building toward — use the strides here (not the easy km) to consciously lift it.
- **Sat 2026-08-01 — 4km Tempo Run** (7.0 km, ~37 min)
  - **Why:** Lactate threshold — raises the pace you can sustain. The single biggest lever for Base-phase fitness at your distance.
  - **Structure:** 10 min easy jog at conversational pace @ 4:35-5:03/km → 4 km tempo at threshold pace @ 4:04-4:18/km → 10 min easy jog to finish @ 4:35-5:03/km
  - **Feel:** Comfortably hard, RPE 7. You could speak a sentence, not hold a chat.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Too hard? Slow 5-10 s/km rather than stopping — the aerobic stimulus survives a slower tempo, not a broken one. Heat or hills: switch to effort.
  - **Form cue:** Lock the rhythm with your breathing (e.g. 2:2) and let pace follow it.
  - **Coach's note:** Your training_load_7day is flagged 'Anaerobic Focus' and most of your indoor HYROX cardio sits at moderate HR (120-148), not truly easy — this tempo is the one deliberate hard effort of the week; let everything else stay genuinely conversational.
- **Sun 2026-08-02 — 5km Long Run** (4.6 km, ~21 min)
  - **Why:** Endurance — time on feet grows capillaries, fat metabolism and durability. Unstructured on purpose: no targets, run to feel, switch off.
  - **Structure:** 4.6 km easy @ 4:35-5:03/km
  - **Feel:** Relaxed, conversational. The watch stays quiet today — that's the point.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Fading late is normal early in a plan; walk breaks beat abandoning. Feeling great ≠ speed up — save it for the structured long runs.
  - **Form cue:** Check posture every ~15min: tall hips, relaxed shoulders, quiet feet.
  - **Coach's note:** Today's training_readiness read 47 (Garmin status: Recovery) and body battery 43 — run the slow edge of the window, and skip any HYROX cardio add-on if one's on the calendar today.
