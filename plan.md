## Rationale

Built for a 1:25:00 half (4:02/km) off a Garmin VO2max of 53.4. The honest picture: the engine supports the goal, but two things in your data don't yet — critical speed sits at 4:08/km and running volume entering the plan was 14.6 km/week. So the plan keeps running at 3 days/week (your 3-4 weekly HYROX/strength sessions carry the rest of the load — pooled CTL 28.9) and buys progress from intensity discipline rather than mileage: only 16.3% of your recent running was genuinely easy, so the single biggest lever is making easy days easy so quality days can be quality. Two 5K time trials (weeks 4 and 8) gate the goal — they either confirm 1:25 fitness or recalibrate every pace honestly. Composite readiness was low at plan acceptance (30.5, overtraining 'caution' after the Salou holiday block), hence the readiness gates written onto every TT and VO2 day.

## Coaching notes

- Easy means easy: only 16% of your recent running was in the easy zone. Easy runs at 4:50-5:03/km with HR under ~140 — if it rates RPE 5 like your Jul 8 run did, it was too hard.
- Cadence is your cheapest speed: you average 155-160 spm with a long stride. Aim 165+ on easy runs and hit the 172-174 targets on rep days — turnover up, reach down.
- Schedule HYROX around the running, not through it: no hard station work within 6h of a quality run, and never the day before tempo/VO2/TT/race-pace days. Your weak stations (Row 1000m, Sandbag Lunges, Sled Pull) are off-day focus work.
- Rate every session's RPE in the portal — strength/HYROX work only counts toward training load through it, and the load model is currently running on a single rated session.
- Heat rule from the Salou block: pace windows assume cool and flat. Above ~20C or on hills, run quality by HR (LT 163) and effort, and let watch pace float.
- The week-4 and week-8 time trials are pace-setters, not workouts: arrive fresh (48h clean), run them honestly, and let recalibrate move the paces — never hand-adjust them.
