## Session detail

### Week 1 — Base (27.4 km)

_Base week 1 of 2: laying aerobic foundation and durable form — economy work now makes every later phase cheaper._

- **Mon 2026-08-10 — 5 x 60s Hill Reps** (7.0 km, ~32 min)
  - **Why:** Strength, power and economy — uphill running loads the posterior chain at low impact and forces a strong knee drive. Right now this is groundwork — economy and resilience for the harder blocks ahead.
  - **Structure:** 10 min easy jog at conversational pace @ 4:50-5:19/km → 5× (60s hard uphill effort / 90s walking rest @ 4:50-5:19/km) → 10 min easy jog to finish @ 4:50-5:19/km
  - **Feel:** Hard but controlled, RPE 8 — effort-based, forget pace on a gradient.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Calves or achilles complaining? Shorten the reps and stay tall — lean from the ankles, not the waist.
  - **Form cue:** Drive the knee, push the ground away, arms compact — and walk the first 20m of each descent before jogging.
  - **Venue:** Find a steady 4-6% gradient that takes 45-75s to climb; grass or trail saves the calves.
- **Wed 2026-08-12 — 4km Tempo Run** (7.0 km, ~37 min)
  - **Why:** Lactate threshold — raises the pace you can sustain. The single biggest lever for Base-phase fitness at your distance. Right now this is groundwork — economy and resilience for the harder blocks ahead.
  - **Structure:** 10 min easy jog at conversational pace @ 4:50-5:19/km → 4 km tempo at threshold pace @ 4:18-4:32/km → 10 min easy jog to finish @ 4:50-5:19/km
  - **Feel:** Comfortably hard, RPE 7. You could speak a sentence, not hold a chat.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Too hard? Slow 5-10 s/km rather than stopping — the aerobic stimulus survives a slower tempo, not a broken one. Heat or hills: switch to effort.
  - **Form cue:** Lock the rhythm with your breathing (e.g. 2:2) and let pace follow it.
- **Fri 2026-08-14 — 13km Long Run** (13.4 km, ~65 min)
  - **Why:** Endurance — time on feet grows capillaries, fat metabolism and durability. Unstructured on purpose: no targets, run to feel, switch off.
  - **Structure:** 13.4 km easy @ 4:50-5:19/km
  - **Feel:** Relaxed, conversational. The watch stays quiet today — that's the point.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Fading late is normal early in a plan; walk breaks beat abandoning. Feeling great ≠ speed up — save it for the structured long runs.
  - **Form cue:** Check posture every ~15min: tall hips, relaxed shoulders, quiet feet.

### Week 2 — Base (30.2 km)

_Base week 2 of 2: laying aerobic foundation and durable form — economy work now makes every later phase cheaper._

- **Mon 2026-08-17 — 10 x 200m Speed Reps** (6.0 km, ~38 min)
  - **Why:** Speed and running economy — short, fast reps recruit fast-twitch fibres and sharpen form, making every slower pace cheaper. Right now this is groundwork — economy and resilience for the harder blocks ahead.
  - **Structure:** 10 min easy jog at conversational pace @ 4:50-5:19/km → 10× (200m fast & relaxed — mile race effort @ 3:41-3:57/km / 60s walking rest @ 4:50-5:19/km) → 10 min easy jog to finish @ 4:50-5:19/km
  - **Feel:** Fast, springy and light — ~95%, never an all-out sprint. Full recovery is part of the design; R work only counts when you're fresh for each rep.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Form breaking (shoulders up, overstriding)? Stop the set — junk reps train junk form. These reps are about quality, never volume.
  - **Form cue:** Think 'fast feet on hot coals' — ground contact short, heels never settling.
  - **Venue:** Track or a measured flat stretch — GPS pace lies over 200-400m, so run these by effort and check splits after.
  - **Warm-up:** After the easy warm-up: 2-3 drills (high knees, A-skips) plus 2-3 strides — fast reps on a cold engine is how calves tear.
- **Wed 2026-08-19 — 9km Easy + 6 Strides** (9.4 km, ~46 min)
  - **Why:** Easy volume plus neuromuscular priming — strides teach fast, relaxed turnover without fatigue, and set up the next quality day.
  - **Structure:** 8.8 km easy @ 4:50-5:19/km → 6× (Stride — fast & relaxed @ 3:41-3:57/km / 45s walking rest @ 4:50-5:19/km)
  - **Feel:** Easy throughout; strides are fast and springy, ~90% effort, never sprinted.
  - **If it goes wrong:** Strides feeling forced or heavy-legged? Cut them — the easy running is the session; strides are a bonus.
  - **Form cue:** Strides are turnover practice: quick light steps, arms driving straight back, eyes up.
- **Fri 2026-08-21 — 15km Progressive Long Run** (14.8 km, ~70 min)
  - **Why:** Endurance plus fatigue-resistance — running strong in the back half is exactly the race-day skill, trained under control.
  - **Structure:** 8.9 km easy @ 4:50-5:19/km → 4.4 km at marathon pace @ 4:30-4:47/km → 1.5 km at threshold pace @ 4:18-4:32/km
  - **Feel:** Patient early, purposeful late. The final segment is honest work, RPE 6-7.
  - **If it goes wrong:** If the faster segments break form, back off to the slow edge of the window — finishing strong matters more than the split.
