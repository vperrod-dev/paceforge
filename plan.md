## Rationale

Built for 3 days/week (Mon/Wed/Sat) over 11 weeks to 2026-09-20. Your VDOT (54.7) already predicts a 1:24:40 half marathon, just ahead of the 1:25:00 goal — so the priority here is durability and consistency, not chasing extra speed. Weekly volume starts at 27km (up from your current ~15km/week) and climbs to a 33.5km peak, with genuine cutback weeks at 4 and 8 (a 3:1 build:deload rhythm) rather than a straight ramp. With only 3 running days, a single long run would normally dominate the week's volume, so the two midweek sessions are sized to keep every long run at 32-37% of the week rather than 45-50%+. Quality work (fartlek, hills, tempo, VO2max, threshold, speed, progressive, race pace) rotates through the Base/Build/Peak phases for variety and progressive overload; race-pace segments are folded into the long run in weeks 7 and 10 to rehearse goal pace under fatigue. Today's readiness (training_readiness 19, HRV Low, status Strained, body battery 2%) is very low, so week 1's first session was swapped from fartlek to easy running — re-check readiness before the Wednesday tempo and run `paceforge adapt` if HRV/battery haven't recovered.

## Coaching notes

- Cadence is flagged Low (152 spm) with a long stride — the week 3 strides session and general cue to shorten stride/increase turnover targets this directly.
- HRV and training readiness are currently low — prioritise sleep and don't hesitate to downgrade a quality day to easy if you wake up flat; `paceforge adapt` will do this automatically as new Garmin data comes in.

- From week 7 on, the long run includes goal-pace kilometres — treat these as pacing practice (even splits, relaxed form), not a time trial.
